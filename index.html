Precautions (WAEC Standard)

Stated in reported speech — first person, past tense.

  1. I ensured that I balanced the metre rule freely on the knife-edge before locating its centre of gravity G, so that the weight of the rule produced no moment about the pivot.
  2. I ensured that I took all position readings with my eye placed vertically above the scale, so as to avoid parallax error.
  3. I ensured that I suspended the metre rule firmly at its centre of gravity G, so that it balanced horizontally on its own before any masses were hung.
  4. I ensured that I suspended the masses gently and allowed the rule to settle before reading the balancing position, so as to avoid oscillation.
  5. I ensured that I used a sharp knife-edge at the pivot to minimise friction.
  6. I ensured that I did not allow the hanging masses to swing and I took readings only after the rule had come to rest.
